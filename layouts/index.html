How I work

I come from game programming — C++, Unity, the kind of work where the thing either runs at 60 frames a second or it doesn't. That habit of caring whether something actually works is what I bring to everything else.

I use AI-assisted tools to move fast, but speed isn't the pitch. The pitch is judgment: knowing what the fast path skips, and fixing it before it ships. On the projects here, that meant catching a leaderboard that trusted the client, an auth setup that only looked gated, and an AI feature that would break the moment someone pasted the wrong thing.

Fast to a working prototype. Careful about the part that matters.
